Rewards Migration Calculator

Enter the user's Rewards Balance and select the applicable migration scenario. The calculator shows each step of the migration logic.

Important Migration Rules

Important: The 70% migration allocation must be calculated first. The 5.0625 compression factor is then applied to that 70% allocation.

Compression formula: Compressed Migration Amount = 70% Migration Allocation ÷ 5.0625

Users who never migrated: calculate 70% of the full Rewards Balance first, then compress that 70% using the 5.0625 factor.

Users who already migrated: first calculate and remove the deposit that generated this specific portion of the Rewards Balance. Then calculate 70% of the Rewards Excluding the Deposit and apply the 5.0625 compression factor to that 70% allocation.

Important: For users who already migrated, do not use all deposits ever made by the user. Only the deposit amount that generated this specific portion of the Rewards Balance should be considered.
